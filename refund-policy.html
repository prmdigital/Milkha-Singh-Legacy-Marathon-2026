Cancellations & Refunds

Last updated: 15 July 2026

Draft for legal review. This policy must be shown to participants before payment is taken. Payment gateways in India require an accessible refund policy before they will approve a merchant account.

Cancellations

  • All registrations are non-refundable.
  • No cancellations will be accepted once registration is completed.
  • Registrations may not be transferred to another participant.

Event Postponement or Cancellation

The event will proceed rain or shine unless extreme weather or safety concerns arise, in which case the organisers reserve the right to postpone or cancel.

In case of cancellation or postponement due to unforeseen circumstances, registration fees will not be refunded but may be adjusted towards a future event organised by the same team.

Registration Fees

Registration fees for each race category are to be confirmed and will be published with the official race details.

Contact

For any question about this policy, email info@milkhasinghlegacymarathon.com.
